=============================================================================
NetworkPolicy is ENABLED - outbound traffic is default-deny
=============================================================================
Each component may only reach: DNS, other pods in this release, and the CIDRs
listed in networkPolicy.egressAllowlist (currently RFC1918 private space
unless you changed it).

If Mongo or Elasticsearch is on a PUBLIC endpoint (Mongo Atlas, Elastic
Cloud), add its CIDR or those components will not connect:

  --set networkPolicy.egressAllowlist[3].cidr=<your.mongo.ip/32>

Enforcement needs a NetworkPolicy-capable CNI (Calico, Cilium, Azure NPM).
Verify:  kubectl get networkpolicy -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}
